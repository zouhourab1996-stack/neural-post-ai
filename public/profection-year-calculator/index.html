How the calculation works

Profected house = (completed age modulo 12) + 1. Age 0 activates the first house; age 1 activates the second; the cycle returns to the first house at ages 12, 24, 36 and so on. Count forward from your natal rising sign in zodiacal order to find the profected sign. Its traditional ruler is the Lord of the Year.

Use the result thoughtfully

A high-profile year lord does not guarantee an outcome, and the technique should not replace practical judgment or professional advice.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my annual profection year?

Divide your completed age by 12, take the remainder and add 1. That gives the activated whole-sign house. Count forward from your rising sign to find the profected sign; its traditional ruler is the Lord of the Year.

Do I need my exact birth time?

You need a reliable rising sign, which is normally calculated from your birth date, exact time and location. This calculator does not calculate the Ascendant.

When does an annual profection year start?

This calculator uses completed age and changes the profection at the birthday. Some astrologers use the exact solar return moment, which can differ by hours.

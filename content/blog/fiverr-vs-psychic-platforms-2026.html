How to compare total cost

For a fixed gig, add the package, rush delivery and optional extras. For a live service, multiply the rate by your planned minutes and include connection or credit fees if applicable. Set a limit before the reading begins. A welcome offer is not the same as the normal price.

Privacy and safety checklist

  • Do not share passwords, financial details or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Keep payment and messages on the platform.
  • Ask whether sessions are recorded and how long data is retained.
  • Leave when a reader claims a curse or demands urgent extra money.
  • Do not use a reading for medical, legal or financial decisions.

Which one should a beginner choose?

Start with a small, clearly described service. A written reading gives you time to review the wording; a live session gives you immediate questions but can create time pressure. Read the three-star reviews and check whether the seller explains limitations rather than promising an exact future.

FAQ

Is Fiverr cheaper than a psychic platform?

Sometimes, but not always. Compare the total package or timed-session cost, not the headline starting price.

Can either service guarantee a reunion?

No. A reader cannot verify another person’s feelings or promise a future event.

What is the safest first purchase?

One focused question, the smallest useful package and no extra payment for curse removal or guaranteed results.

The last full moon of 2026 lands at the busiest, darkest point of the year. It comes two days after the winter solstice and right on top of Christmas Eve, and it's the closest full moon of the whole year. If you're in North America, it turns full on the evening of Wednesday, December 23. In the UK and Europe, it peaks in the small hours of December 24.

Two things jump out. In every northern city the Moon rises before the Sun sets, so you get a few minutes of "moonrise at golden hour", which is the best photo light of the night. And the further north you are, the longer it stays: nearly 22 hours above the horizon in Anchorage. In Sydney the opposite happens. It's midsummer there, so the full moon is low and brief.

How to get the best view

  • Catch the rise. Find a spot with a clear eastern-northeastern horizon and be there 10 minutes before your moonrise time. Near the horizon the Moon looks enormous and orange. That's the Moon illusion plus the thick air, not the supermoon effect, but it pairs well with it.
  • Look again near midnight. The Moon is almost overhead in the southern US and very high everywhere else. It's bright enough to cast sharp shadows on snow.
  • Photograph it with something in front. A tree line, a steeple or a skyline gives scale. Expose for the Moon, not the landscape, or it turns into a white blob.

The Ursid meteors peak on December 22, but this Moon washes most of them out. The better shower this month is the Geminids on December 13–14.

Why It's the Biggest Supermoon of 2026

"Supermoon" isn't an astronomy term. The astrologer Richard Nolle coined it in 1979 for a new or full moon that happens when the Moon is near its closest point to Earth. Astronomers call the same thing a perigean full moon. The lists vary because people use different cut-offs. Under the common 90% rule popularised by Fred Espenak's tables, 2026 has three full supermoons: January 3, November 24 and December 24.

Compared with the year's farthest full moon, the Cold Moon is about 12–14% larger across and up to about 30% brighter. Next to an average full moon the difference is closer to 7%, which few people can see without a side-by-side photo. What you will notice is the brightness on snow and how high the Moon rides.

Why It's Called the Cold Moon (and Its Other Names)

The name most US calendars use, Cold Moon, is listed by the Old Farmer's Almanac as a Mohawk name for the December full moon. Several other names point to the same few weeks of the year:

  • Long Night Moon (Mohican): it rises during the longest nights and stays up the longest.
  • Moon Before Yule: an old European name, because it usually falls just before the midwinter festival of Yule.
  • Snow Moon (Haida, Cherokee), Hoar Frost Moon and Drift Clearing Moon (Cree), Moon of the Popping Trees (Oglala), after the sound of sap freezing in trunks.
  • Moon When the Deer Shed Their Antlers (Dakota) and Little Spirit Moon (Anishinaabe).

In 2026 the Moon Before Yule name almost fits too well. The full moon lands within three days of the solstice and on Christmas Eve itself in UTC.

The Cancer–Capricorn axis: home versus the world

The Moon rules Cancer, so traditional astrology calls a Cancer full moon one where the Moon is "at home". Cancer is about family, the past, where you feel safe and who you feed. Capricorn, where the Sun sits, is about work, status, responsibility and the long game. A full moon across this axis tends to light up the gap between the two. That fits a week when many people trade the office for a crowded family kitchen.

The Neptune square: a T-square in cardinal signs

The detail most generic horoscopes miss is Neptune. At 1°38′ Aries it sits almost exactly at right angles to both the Moon and the Sun, forming what astrologers call a T-square, with Neptune at the apex. Neptune is linked to idealism, nostalgia, blurred boundaries and escapism. Square to a Cancer moon, it's the classic picture of the "perfect holiday" fantasy meeting the real family. It also hints at the wish to numb out with one more drink, one more scroll or one more episode.

Neptune is also compassion and imagination. A useful question for this full moon: which family story am I still carrying that isn't true anymore? Saturn, also in early Aries, adds that boundaries set kindly now tend to hold.

Right after the solstice

The Sun entered Capricorn on December 21 (see our winter solstice 2026 guide), so this is the first lunation of the new season: a checkpoint between the plans of Sagittarius season and the structures Capricorn asks you to build.

Simple Cold Moon Rituals That Actually Fit the Week

No crystals required. Five practices that suit a Cancer full moon and take under half an hour:

  1. The moonrise pause (10 minutes). Step outside at your moonrise time from the table above. No phone. Watch the Moon clear the horizon and notice how you feel about the year ending. That's it.
  2. Write a "keep, release, carry" list. Three columns: what you're keeping from 2026, what you're releasing, and what you're carrying into 2027. Cancer is about memory, so be honest about the third column.
  3. Cook something from your family's past. A grandparent's recipe, a dish from where you grew up. Feeding people is the most Cancerian act there is.
  4. A boundary sentence. Because of the Neptune–Saturn background, write one kind, clear sentence you can use if a family conversation goes somewhere you don't want it to. You'll be glad you prepared it.

FAQ

When is the Cold Moon in December 2026?

The December 2026 full moon is exact on December 24 at 01:28 UTC. That's Wednesday, December 23, at 8:28 p.m. Eastern, 5:28 p.m. Pacific, and 1:28 a.m. GMT on Thursday, December 24, in the UK.

Is the December 2026 full moon a supermoon?

Yes. At about 356,740 km (221,667 miles), it's the closest full moon of 2026 and the third supermoon of the year under the common 90% definition. Perigee follows about seven hours after the full moon.

What sign is the December 2026 full moon in?

Cancer, at 2°13′, opposite the Sun at 2°14′ Capricorn. The Moon enters Cancer about three and a half hours before it turns full and stays there until about 21:13 UTC on December 25.

What is the best night to see the Cold Moon?

Wednesday, December 23, from moonrise around sunset onwards. The Moon also looks full on December 24. It rides very high around midnight and stays above the horizon about 15 to 19 hours in most US, Canadian and UK cities.

Why is it called the Cold Moon?

The Old Farmer's Almanac lists Cold Moon as a Mohawk name for the December full moon. Other names include Long Night Moon (Mohican), Moon Before Yule, Snow Moon and Moon of the Popping Trees.

What does the full moon in Cancer mean spiritually?

Astrologers link a Cancer full moon with home, family, memory and emotional security, set against Capricorn's work and responsibility. In 2026 a close square from Neptune adds nostalgia and idealism, which makes it a good moment to separate the family you have from the one you imagine. This is a reflective reading, not a prediction.

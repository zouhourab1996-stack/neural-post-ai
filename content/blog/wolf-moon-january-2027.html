Wolf Moon January 2027: The Short Answer

The Wolf Moon is exact on Friday, January 22, 2027, at 12:17 UTC. That is 7:17 a.m. in New York and 4:17 a.m. in Los Angeles. The Moon sits at about 2 degrees of Leo, opposite the Sun in Aquarius.

It is also the closest full moon of 2027, so it counts as a supermoon. Below you will find the time in your zone, what you will really see in the sky, and a short reading for every sun sign.

Is the Wolf Moon Really a Supermoon?

Yes, by the common definition. The Moon is about 357,600 km from Earth, which is inside the usual 360,000 km line. No other full moon in 2027 comes closer.

Here is the honest part. The Moon's distance ranges from about 356,500 km at its closest to 406,700 km at its farthest. The Wolf Moon sits near the close end. Side by side with the smallest full moon of 2027, it would look about 10 to 14 percent wider. On its own in the sky, you will probably not notice.

How to Get the Best Look

  • Check your local moonrise time. A full moon rises close to sunset.
  • Find a clear view to the east or northeast, such as a hill, a beach or a roof.
  • Go out as it clears the horizon. It looks biggest and orange then. This is the moon illusion, not extra size.
  • Dress warm. January evenings in the north are cold, and the moon stays high all night.
  • Use a phone with a tripod or a steady wall, and tap the moon to lower the exposure.

Why Is It Called the Wolf Moon?

The name comes from the traditional list of full moon names, popular in North America. It refers to the deep winter, when wolves were said to howl near villages. Other names for January's moon include Ice Moon and Old Moon.

These names follow the northern seasons. In Sydney or Cape Town, the same moon rises in the middle of summer. The names are folklore, and they have no scientific meaning.

FAQ: Wolf Moon January 2027

When is the full moon in January 2027?

On Friday, January 22, 2027, at 12:17 UTC. That is 7:17 a.m. Eastern, 4:17 a.m. Pacific and 12:17 p.m. in London on the same date. In Sydney it is 11:17 p.m. on January 22.

Is the January 2027 Wolf Moon a supermoon?

Yes. At about 357,600 km from Earth it is the closest full moon of 2027. There is no official rule, but most sources call a full moon a supermoon when it is within roughly 360,000 km.

What zodiac sign is the January 2027 full moon in?

Leo, at about 2 degrees 14 minutes. The Sun is opposite it at the same degree of Aquarius.

Why is it called the Wolf Moon?

It is the traditional name for January's full moon in the northern hemisphere. It comes from folklore about hungry wolves howling in midwinter. It is a naming tradition, not a scientific term.

Will the Wolf Moon look much bigger?

Only a little. A supermoon can look about 7 to 14 percent larger than average or smallest full moons, which is hard to notice by eye. It looks biggest right after moonrise, thanks to the moon illusion.

Is there an eclipse with this full moon?

No. The next eclipse is the annular solar eclipse at the new moon on February 6, 2027. A penumbral lunar eclipse follows at the February 20 full moon.

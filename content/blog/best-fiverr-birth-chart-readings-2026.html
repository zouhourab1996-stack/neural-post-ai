Why your birth time is not optional

The rising sign — your ascendant — changes roughly every two hours, because it is simply the zodiac sign rising on the eastern horizon at the moment you were born. Get the time wrong by three hours and the ascendant, the house placements, and therefore most of the reading, are wrong too.

Can't find your birth time? Ask the hospital or check a birth certificate or family record first — it is often written down somewhere. If it is genuinely lost, ask the reader for a solar chart (houses set by the Sun's position) rather than paying for rectification. Be sceptical of anyone who charges extra to "recover" your time.

What a real reading should cover

  • The big three: Sun (core drive), Moon (emotional wiring), Ascendant (how you come across).
  • House placements — which life areas the planets activate. This is where generic readings fall apart.
  • Aspects — the angles between planets; the conjunctions, squares and trines that describe tension and ease.
  • At least one specific application — career, relationships, or the current transits affecting you now.
  • A file you keep: chart image, PDF, or recording, plus a written summary.

If a $12 gig promises "full natal chart with all aspects and houses explained", the economics are telling you it is a template. That is fine if you know that is what you are buying — the problem is only when it is sold as personal interpretation.

How to vet the seller

  • Ask what software or method they use and whether the chart image is included. A real astrologer answers in one line.
  • Ask what they need from you. If they do not ask for birth time, they are not calculating houses — full stop.
  • Check reviews for specifics: "she explained my 12th-house stellium and why work feels hidden" is a real review.
  • Look for transit work in the packages — applying the chart to now is the hard part, and it is what separates practitioners from hobbyists.

What if I don't know my birth time?

Ask for a solar chart, which sets houses by the Sun's position and does not need a time. Be wary of "rectification" upsells — recovering an unknown birth time is not something a reader can reliably do from your life events.

Is a birth chart the same as a horoscope?

No. A horoscope is written for a sun sign — one twelfth of the population. A natal chart is calculated for one exact moment and place, and it is unique to you.

What's the difference between natal, transit and synastry readings?

Natal is the chart you are born with. Transits compare today's sky to that chart — what is being activated now. Synastry overlays two people's charts, which is why it costs more.

How long should a good reading take?

20–40 minutes of content for a natal chart, or a well-structured PDF of a few thousand words. Much shorter than that and whole areas are being skipped.

Can astrology tell me when to change jobs or get married?

A reader can describe the character of a period — what kind of pressure or opening a transit tends to bring. Turning that into "quit on 14 March" is not something a responsible astrologer will do.

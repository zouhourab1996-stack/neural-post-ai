A 5-minute vetting checklist

  • Read three reviews that mention specifics. "She explained why my chart's 10th house mattered for the job question" beats fifty × "amazing!!!".
  • Check the scope is written down. Good gigs state: how many questions, what format, delivery time, and whether revisions or follow-ups are included.
  • Look at the packages, not the headline price. The $5 tier is usually a hook; the useful scope sits in Standard.
  • Ask one question before ordering. "Do you need my birth time, or just my question?" A fast, specific reply is a strong signal.
  • Confirm the refund position. Fiverr's own resolution centre handles disputes — but only for orders placed on the platform. Never move a Fiverr conversation to WhatsApp or PayPal.
Stay on platform. Every protection you have — delivery guarantee, dispute process, verified reviews — exists only inside Fiverr. A seller who offers a "discount for direct payment" is removing your safety net, not adding value.

Before you order: get the question right

Readers work with what you give them. "Will I be happy?" produces filler. "I have two job offers — one pays more, one is closer to family; what should I weigh?" produces a usable reading. Are Fiverr psychic readings legitimate?

The platform is legitimate, the sellers vary. Fiverr verifies accounts, publishes unfiltered reviews, and holds payment until delivery — which is more consumer protection than most psychic sites offer. Whether a reading is meaningful is a personal judgement no marketplace can guarantee.

How much should I spend on my first reading?

$10–$35. That buys a recorded reading in the 10–20 minute range, which is long enough to address two or three real questions and short enough that a disappointing session costs you little.

Chat, recorded video, or live session?

Chat is cheapest and leaves you a transcript you can re-read. Recorded video is the best value — you keep the file and can rewatch it when the meaning lands later. Live is worth it only once you already trust the reader.

Can I get a refund if the reading feels generic?

Fiverr's resolution centre can cancel an order for non-delivery or a scope that was never met. It will not refund you for "didn't resonate" — so read the package description carefully and ask a question before ordering.

Will a reader tell me about my health or a death?

Reputable readers decline medical, legal, and death predictions — both as an ethics line and because Fiverr's terms restrict them. Treat any reading as entertainment and reflection, never as medical or financial advice.

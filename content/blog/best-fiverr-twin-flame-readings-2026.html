What a useful reading should include

  • The format and approximate length.
  • How many questions you can ask.
  • What information the reader needs, and why.
  • A delivery time and a revision or follow-up policy.
  • A clear statement that the reading is spiritual reflection, not certainty.

The red flags that matter most

Leave when a seller says you have a curse, a blockage or a dark energy that only they can remove for an extra payment. Also leave when the package promises a specific reunion date, demands private payment outside Fiverr, or tells you to ignore friends, therapy or safety concerns.

Ask a better question

“Will they come back?” gives you a prediction you cannot verify. Try: “What pattern in this connection should I reflect on, and what boundary would protect me?” A better question does not make the reading true; it makes the experience more useful and less likely to turn into a search for certainty.

How to read reviews

Read the three-star reviews first. Look for details about delivery, specificity and whether the seller respected the brief. A long list of “amazing” comments does not tell you whether the reading answered a real question. Keep payment and messages on Fiverr so the platform’s resolution process remains available.

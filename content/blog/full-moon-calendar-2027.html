Key Takeaways

  • 2027 has 12 full moons, one a month. The first is the Wolf Moon on January 22 at 12:17 UTC and the last is the Cold Moon on December 13 at 16:08 UTC.
  • The Wolf Moon is the supermoon of the year. It is also the closest full moon of 2027, at about 357,600 km. The July and August full moons are the smallest.
  • Three penumbral lunar eclipses (February 20, July 18, August 17) are real but subtle. Only February's is worth going outside for, and only if you know what to look for.
  • Four dates shift by a day depending on where you live: February 20, June 19, September 15 and November 14. The table below shows six cities so you can see your own date.
  • Astrologically, the year walks the Leo-Aquarius axis and ends in Gemini-Sagittarius. The month-by-month notes below say what each moon is actually good for.

Month by Month: What Each Full Moon Is For

Astrology reads a full moon as a culmination: something you started around the new moon two weeks earlier becomes visible. The sign tells you which area of life gets the spotlight, and the Sun sits in the opposite sign, so every full moon is a tug between two ideas. This is a reflective tool, not a forecast. I have kept each note practical. If you want the ritual side, my new moon ritual guide works just as well flipped around for a full moon release.

January 22: Wolf Moon in Leo (Sun in Aquarius)

Big, bright and the closest of the year. Leo asks what you are proud of; Aquarius asks who it is for. It comes two weeks after the January 7 new moon in Capricorn and twelve days after Mars turns retrograde, so the honest question is which goal you set on the 7th now looks different. Good for finishing a creative project, bad for confronting someone you love while tired.

February 20: Snow Moon in Virgo (Sun in Pisces)

The busiest week of the year. Mars is at opposition on February 19 in Virgo, a penumbral eclipse falls on the 20th right beside it, and Mars slips back into Leo on the 21st. Virgo wants a list; Pisces wants a nap. Do the boring admin you have been dodging, then rest without guilt. The full breakdown is in my Mars retrograde 2027 guide.

March 22: Worm Moon in Libra (Sun in Aries)

The first full moon of spring and the Paschal full moon, which is why Western Easter lands on Sunday, March 28. Libra against Aries is the classic "me versus us" axis. Use it to settle a negotiation you have been avoiding, not to start a fight you can win.

April 20: Pink Moon in Scorpio (Sun in Taurus)

Barely into Scorpio, at 1 degree. Taurus holds on to what is safe, Scorpio wants the buried thing dug up. Money, shared resources and trust are the usual themes. Check a joint account or a contract you have not read in a year.

May 20: Flower Moon in Scorpio (Sun in Gemini)

Scorpio again, this time at 29 degrees, the last degree of the sign. Astrologers call the final degree an "anaretic" point and read it as urgency. I read it more plainly: the second Scorpio full moon in a month is a second chance to finish what the April one exposed. It is also a seasonal Blue Moon, the third of four full moons in the spring season.

June 19: Strawberry Moon in Sagittarius (Sun in Gemini)

Gemini against Sagittarius is information versus meaning: lots of facts, not enough direction. Close to the June solstice, the Moon rides low in the sky from the northern hemisphere, which makes it look huge and amber at moonrise. A trip planned now works better than a trip planned next week.

July 18: Buck Moon in Capricorn (Sun in Cancer)

The farthest full moon of the year, a micromoon, and a mild one emotionally. Cancer asks for comfort, Capricorn for results. A good moment to look at family responsibilities honestly. The penumbral eclipse is marginal and mostly seen from Asia and Australia.

August 17: Sturgeon Moon in Aquarius (Sun in Leo)

A second micromoon and a penumbral eclipse, two weeks after the August 2 total solar eclipse that crosses Spain, Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya and Egypt. Leo versus Aquarius repeats January's theme, so check what you decided back then. If you plan to watch the eclipse, read my solar eclipse 2027 guide first.

September 15: Harvest Moon in Pisces (Sun in Virgo)

The full moon closest to the September equinox on September 23, which gives it that famous "same time every evening" rising pattern in the north. Virgo and Pisces is the sharpest version of detail versus intuition. Pick one, then check it against the other.

October 15: Hunter's Moon in Aries (Sun in Libra)

The Harvest Moon's follow-up, with the moon rising a little later each night. Aries is impatient and Libra is polite, which means this is the full moon where unspoken resentment gets spoken. Choose your words before you send the message.

November 14: Beaver Moon in Taurus (Sun in Scorpio)

Taurus is the Moon's sign of exaltation, so this one is steadier than it looks. Good for money decisions, food, sleep and the body. If you track how you feel around full moons, the data in my full moon and sleep article will help you read your own pattern honestly.

December 13: Cold Moon in Gemini (Sun in Sagittarius)

The last of the year and the same night as the peak of the Geminid meteor shower. Gemini and Sagittarius ask the same year-end question: what did you learn, and will you say it out loud? The Moon is bright, so plan for fewer meteors than in 2026. For the shower itself see my Geminid guide, and for 2026's own Cold Moon see the Cold Moon post.

The Three Eclipses, Honestly Rated

A penumbral lunar eclipse means the Moon passes through the pale outer part of Earth's shadow. It does not turn red and it does not lose a bite. At best the Moon looks a little dull on one edge. Here is how I would rate the three.

  • February 20, maximum about 23:12 UTC. Visible from the Americas, Europe and Africa. Of the three, this is the only one I would plan around, and mostly because Mars is shining nearby. Compare a photo from an hour earlier with one at maximum and you will see the shading.
  • July 18, maximum about 16:02 UTC. Marginal. Seen from Asia, Australia and eastern Russia, and the dimming is too slight for most eyes.
  • August 17. Another penumbral event, with the Moon at a micromoon distance. At 07:28 UTC the Moon is up over the Americas and the Pacific, so that is where to look, and it is still subtle to the naked eye.

Astrologers still mark all three because the Moon is near a lunar node. I would not make a decision on that alone. If an eclipse month feels messy, it probably is: it is also retrograde season in February.

Supermoons, Micromoons and the Blue Moon

The Moon's orbit is an ellipse, so its distance from Earth swings from about 357,000 km to 406,000 km. A full moon near the close end is a supermoon, and one near the far end is a micromoon. The difference is up to about 14 percent in apparent width. That is real in a photo and almost invisible to the eye, because there is nothing next to the Moon for scale.

There is no single official definition, which explains why calendars disagree. By the strictest rule, only January 22 is a supermoon in 2027. A looser rule that counts anything within about 90 percent of the closest approach in that orbit also catches the February 20 moon, at 363,306 km. The July 18 and August 17 moons are the micromoons, both near 405,000 km.

The May 20 Flower Moon is a "seasonal" Blue Moon: the third of four full moons between the March equinox and the June solstice. There is no calendar-month Blue Moon in 2027. August 31 is a "Black Moon" (a second new moon in one month), which is a name that mostly exists for headlines.

Where the Names Come From

Wolf, Snow, Worm, Pink, Flower, Strawberry, Buck, Sturgeon, Harvest, Hunter's, Beaver and Cold are the names most U.S. almanacs use today. Most trace back to Native American and colonial-era seasonal naming, and different peoples used different names for the same moon. The Harvest Moon is the one name tied to a rule: it is the full moon nearest the September equinox, which is why in some years it falls in October. In 2027 it is September 15. Treat the rest as friendly labels for the season, not as science.

How I Would Use This Calendar

Here is the plan I would follow rather than trying to mark every moon.

  1. Pick three moons, not twelve. January 22 for an honest look at the year ahead, September 15 for a harvest review, December 13 for closing the year.
  2. Put the date in your own city's time, using the table above. If the moment is near midnight, mark both evenings.
  3. Look at the sign of your Sun, Moon and rising. A full moon at 2 degrees Leo matters more if you have planets near 2 degrees of a fixed sign. If you do not know your rising sign, my big three guide shows how to find it.
  4. Write one sentence at moonrise. What has become obvious in the last two weeks? That is the entire practice.
  5. Do the practical thing the same week. Gardeners can match the planting notes in gardening by the moon.

If you only act on one thing this year, make it the Wolf Moon. It is the closest and brightest, and it arrives at the start of the year when you still have time to use what you notice.

FAQ

When is the first full moon of 2027?

The Wolf Moon on Friday, January 22, 2027 at 12:17 UTC. That is 7:17 a.m. in New York, 12:17 p.m. in London and 9:17 p.m. in Tokyo.

How many full moons are there in 2027?

Twelve, one in each month. There is no calendar-month Blue Moon, but the May 20 full moon is a seasonal Blue Moon, the third of four in its season.

Is there a supermoon in 2027?

Yes. January 22 is the closest full moon of the year at about 357,600 km. February 20 (363,306 km) also counts under looser definitions. Calendars disagree because there is no official rule.

Are there lunar eclipses in 2027?

Three, and all are penumbral: February 20, July 18 and August 17. They only dim the Moon slightly. The February event is visible from the Americas, Europe and Africa.

Which full moon is the Harvest Moon in 2027?

September 15 at 23:03 UTC, the full moon closest to the September equinox on September 23.

Why do full moon dates differ between websites?

The exact moment is the same everywhere, but the calendar date depends on your time zone. A full moon at 23:24 UTC on February 20 is February 21 in Dubai, Tokyo and Sydney. A difference of one minute between sources is also normal.

What sign is each full moon in during 2027?

Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces, Aries, Taurus and Gemini, in order from January to December (tropical zodiac).

When is Easter in 2027?

Western Easter is March 28, 2027, the first Sunday after the Paschal full moon on March 22.

What a Jupiter retrograde actually is (and why it is not rare)

The astronomy is the easy part. Jupiter does not reverse. It appears to, because we watch it from a planet that overtakes it on the inside track. Earth laps Jupiter roughly every thirteen months, and for about four months either side of the overtaking — while our line of sight swings past — the big planet seems to back up through the zodiac. Draw Jupiter's position against the fixed stars every night for a year and you get a loop: forward, slow, stop, backwards, slow, stop, forward. Every planet outside Earth's orbit draws that loop. Jupiter's is just the widest and the calmest.

The numbers for this particular loop: Jupiter backs up from 27°03′ Leo to 17°01′ Leo — ten degrees and two arc-minutes — over 121 days. Its fastest backwards speed, reached in mid-January, is about a tenth of a degree per day. For comparison, Mercury retrograde at its swiftest covers that much sky in a single afternoon. This is one reason Jupiter retrogrades feel like a mood rather than an incident: nothing outpaces you fast enough to be an incident.

Now the part that recalibrates the whole topic: this happens constantly. Jupiter is retrograde about 120 days out of every 399 — we clocked it at 29.7% of all days between 2000 and 2030. Pick a stranger at random and there is roughly a three-in-ten chance Jupiter was retrograde the day they were born. There is a retrograde of some outer planet happening, or about to happen, for most of your life. If retrograde Jupiter truly cancelled anything, civilisation would have noticed by now.

What the tradition actually says is narrower and more interesting: Jupiter is the principle of expansion — growth, luck, meaning, the bigger yes. When it retrogrades, the expansion turns inward. The outer wins slow down; the inner accounting speeds up. Astrologers who work with this cycle treat it as a review period for anything that got big fast: faith, plans, promises, purchases, philosophies, the identity you built during the direct phase. Not a punishment. An audit.

What it means: the audit of the summer's growth

Here is the specific shape of this audit, and why it is worth more attention than the average Jupiter retrograde.

Jupiter entered Leo on June 30, 2026, for a thirteen-month stay — its first since July 2014 to August 2015, and its last until June 2038. Leo is the sign of the creative self: what you make, how you shine, the risky business of being seen as yourself rather than as a role. Jupiter in Leo is the twelve-year window when that part of life gets funding. The first phase, June 30 to December 12, has been the outbound phase: new projects, new visibility, new confidence, new commitments made in the flush of it. The retrograde from December 13 to April 13 is the inbound phase: Jupiter walks back over every degree it covered between mid-September and December, re-examining what got built there.

Practically: any opportunity, project, relationship, purchase or promise that opened up between July and early December 2026 comes up for a second look between December and April. Some of it passes and gets bigger in 2027. Some of it turns out to have been performance — yours or someone else's — and quietly deflates. The retrograde does not usually tear anything down; it just stops inflating it, and you find out what was holding its own weight. If something falls away over this winter, the honest reading is that it was gravity, not sabotage.

Because Jupiter is involved, the review is about meaning more than logistics. The questions this cycle asks are not "did the emails send" but: Is the work I am becoming known for actually mine? Did I say yes to this because I believe it or because it applauded? Is the bigger life I am building bigger in the directions I actually care about? A retrograde in Leo makes those questions personal rather than abstract. It is one thing to audit your beliefs; it is another to audit your persona.

One more framing that helps: the winter of 2026–27 is a stacked review season, not a one-off. Venus retrograde ran its course in Scorpio and Libra from October 3 to November 14 (see the Venus retrograde guide), Mercury retrograde overlapped it from October 24 to November 13 in Scorpio (see the Mercury retrograde guide), and now the slowest, gentlest member of the family takes the baton for four months. By the time Jupiter stations direct in April, everything from your relationships to your emails to your beliefs will have been through a second draft. The spring is unusually well edited. That is the payoff.

The echo: December 2014 – April 2015

Jupiter returns to each sign every twelve years, and its retrogrades land in suspiciously similar places. The last Jupiter retrograde in Leo ran December 8, 2014 (station at 22°35′) to April 8, 2015 (station at 12°33′). This one runs December 13, 2026 to April 13, 2027, five degrees higher in the sign. The pattern is not exact, but it is close enough to be useful: whatever house Leo occupies in your chart now, it occupied then too.

So do the unglamorous, high-yield thing: go back to that winter in your own life. What did you commit to between December 2014 and April 2015? What got scaled back, admitted, renegotiated, given up? For the cohort that was a teenager then, this retrograde is the first adult rerun of a story whose first draft you lived at fourteen or fifteen. For everyone else, 2014–15 is the template: the themes that surfaced then — creative confidence, recognition, the question of who gets to be seen — surface again now, at twelve years' more distance from the original wound and twelve years' closer to the answer.

And if you want the wider arc: the retrograde before that was in Leo's neighbour — Jupiter was retrograde in Cancer into early March 2026 — and the one after this will run January 12 to May 13, 2028, entirely in Virgo. The audit migrates one sign, and one house of your chart, roughly every thirteen months. You can, in principle, keep one clean window per year for the rest of your life.

The part nobody else tells you: the Saturn trine at the direct station

Search "Jupiter retrograde 2026" and you will find the dates, the crystals, and twelve nearly identical paragraphs about reviewing your beliefs. What none of the ranking pages mention — we checked — is the most useful fact of the whole cycle, and it sits at the end of it.

When Jupiter turns direct on April 13, 2027, it is already inside a trine to Saturn — exact on April 3 at 17°10′ Leo–Aries, re-exact on July 12 at 27°15′, and within a 3° orb from late March through late July. Expansion (Jupiter) and structure (Saturn), in the two fire signs, agreeing with each other for four straight months.

This is the rare and desirable geometry: growth that has passed its audit being handed, at the moment it resumes, directly to the planet that builds things to last. In practice, astrologers read a Jupiter–Saturn trine as the "sustainable expansion" aspect — the promotion with the workload priced in, the project funded on real numbers, the yes that survives contact with the calendar. That this corridor wraps the direct station means the things Jupiter re-approved over the winter get a structural runway from mid-April onward.

The sequencing is worth marking in a calendar. First exact pass: April 3 — the deal is drafted. Direct station: April 13 — the growth resumes. Second exact pass: July 12 — the deal is finalised, one day after the post-shadow closes on July 11. Then Jupiter leaves Leo on July 26 and the whole twelve-year chapter in your Leo house closes until 2038. In other words: April to July 2027 is the collection window — the stretch where the audited, trine-to-Saturn version of your Jupiter-in-Leo year gets converted into things you keep.

Frequently asked questions

When exactly does Jupiter turn retrograde in 2026?

December 13, 2026, at 01:39 UTC — which is 8:39 PM on December 12 in New York, 5:39 PM in Los Angeles, 2:39 AM on December 13 in Paris, and 1:39 PM in Sydney. US-based sites list December 12; the rest of the world lists December 13. Both are the same moment.

When does Jupiter go direct again?

April 13, 2027, at 02:50 UTC — 10:50 PM on April 12 in New York. The retrograde lasts 121 days, from 27°03′ Leo back to 17°01′ Leo, never leaving the sign.

What are the shadow periods?

The pre-retrograde shadow began September 17, 2026, when Jupiter first crossed 17°01′ Leo — the degree it would later return to. The post-retrograde shadow ends July 11, 2027, when Jupiter re-crosses 27°03′ Leo for the third and final time. Shadow to shadow the cycle runs 297 days.

Is Jupiter retrograde bad luck?

No. Jupiter is retrograde about 30% of the time — four months out of every thirteen — so if retrograde Jupiter destroyed anything, it would be the most reliably destructive force in the calendar. The traditional reading is that expansion turns inward: outer growth slows while beliefs, plans and commitments get reviewed. Things that were over-inflated deflate; things with substance keep growing, more quietly.

How is Jupiter retrograde different from Mercury retrograde?

Speed and subject matter. Mercury retrograde lasts about three weeks and tends to show up as logistics: messages, travel, contracts, devices. Jupiter retrograde lasts about four months and works on meaning: beliefs, growth, generosity, the big commitments. Mercury retrograde is an outage; Jupiter retrograde is an audit.

Was I born with Jupiter retrograde?

Roughly 3 in 10 people were (we measured 29.7% of days between 2000 and 2030). Enter your birth date in the calculator on this page and it will compute Jupiter's exact position and speed at your birth and tell you — along with whether it was stationary, which about one birth in a thousand is close enough to count as.

What does Jupiter retrograde in Leo mean for Leos?

Leo Suns and risings have Jupiter crossing their first house this year, so the retrograde audits identity itself: the bolder self you have been building since June gets a mid-term exam from December to April. Leos born with Jupiter between 17° and 27° of the sign are having a Jupiter return under revision at the same time — the calculator flags it.

Can I launch a project or get married during Jupiter retrograde?

Yes. The tradition's caution is about scale and sequencing rather than permission: launches you want maximum immediate expansion for fare better in the direct windows (before December 12, 2026, or after April 13, 2027 — the spring window, with Saturn trine Jupiter, is the stronger one). Things begun under the retrograde tend to grow more slowly and more durably. Plenty of people marry under retrograde Jupiter and stay married; the classic advice is simply to re-read the terms.

What was happening the last time Jupiter was retrograde in Leo?

December 8, 2014 to April 8, 2015, from 22°35′ Leo back to 12°33′. Look at what you committed to, renegotiated or gave up that winter: with Leo in the same house of your chart then as now, those themes are the first draft of this one.

When is the next Jupiter retrograde after this?

January 12 to May 13, 2028, entirely in Virgo (27°33′ back to 17°34′). Jupiter's next retrograde in Leo is not until December 2038 – April 2039.

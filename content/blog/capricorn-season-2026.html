Capricorn Season 2026-2027 at a Glance

Capricorn season begins on Monday, December 21, 2026, at 20:50 UTC, the exact moment of the December solstice, when the Sun enters Capricorn. It ends on Wednesday, January 20, 2027, at 07:30 UTC, when the Sun moves into Aquarius. That is a season of about 29 days and 11 hours.

This is the season most people use to reset. The holidays are behind you, the new year is in front of you, and the days are slowly getting longer in the Northern Hemisphere. Capricorn is the sign of structure, patience and long-term goals, so astrologers read this month as a time to build, not to rush.

Below you will find every key date with times in UTC, Eastern and Pacific time, what each event is traditionally linked to, a short guide for every rising sign, and a simple plan you can follow. No scare stories and no promises that a planet will fix your life.

Quick answer

What Capricorn Season Means in Astrology

Capricorn is a cardinal earth sign ruled by Saturn. In plain words, it starts things in a practical way. Earth signs care about what is real and measurable: money, work, health, home and time. Saturn adds the lessons of limits, responsibility and patience.

That is why the season is often called the "goal-setting" season. After the emotional pull of Sagittarius season, which is about big dreams and travel, Capricorn asks a harder question: What are you willing to do every day to make that dream real?

Sagittarius picks the mountain. Capricorn packs the bag and starts walking.

Common Themes Astrologers Mention

  • Structure: calendars, budgets, routines and checklists feel more useful than usual.
  • Career: Capricorn is linked to the tenth house of public life, so work goals take center stage.
  • Boundaries: saying no to what drains you is seen as a Saturn-style skill.
  • Patience: small wins add up. Big leaps are not the point.
  • Responsibility: a good month to finish tasks you have been putting off.

The Sky Month by Month: What Happens When

December 21-31: Solstice, Cold Moon and Mercury Moves In

The season opens with the shortest day of the year in the Northern Hemisphere. Our winter solstice 2026 guide has daylight hours for 12 cities and the astronomy behind the date.

Three days later comes the Cold Moon in Cancer, a full moon that falls on the evening of December 23 in the Americas. Cancer is the sign of home and family, so this full moon often lands on holiday gatherings. Astrologers read it as emotional, warm and sometimes tense.

On December 25, Mercury enters Capricorn. Thinking and talking become more serious. It is a good week to write down your goals, because your words and your plans match well.

January 1-10: The New Year Reset

On New Year's Day the Sun and Mercury line up at 11 degrees of Capricorn. That conjunction is a classic "clear head" moment. Use it to review your finances, your calendar and your habits.

On January 7 two things happen. In the morning Venus moves into Sagittarius, which lightens the mood in love and money. In the afternoon in New York (evening in Europe), the New Moon in Capricorn arrives at 17 degrees. This is the main intention-setting date of the season.

Three days later, on January 10, Mars turns retrograde at 10 degrees of Virgo. Mars is the planet of drive and action. A retrograde Mars is traditionally a time to refine plans rather than launch loud new projects. Our Mars retrograde 2027 guide explains how long it lasts and what to expect.

January 11-20: Mercury Leaves, Aquarius Arrives

Mercury enters Aquarius on January 13, a week before the Sun does. Conversations turn toward ideas, communities and the future. When the Sun enters Aquarius on January 20, the mood shifts from "build it" to "share it and improve it."

Good to know: Mercury is not retrograde during Capricorn season. The first Mercury retrograde of 2027 begins in February. Check our Mercury retrograde guide for how these periods work.

The New Moon in Capricorn on January 7, 2027

The new moon is the best-known event of the season. It happens at 20:24 UTC at about 17 degrees of Capricorn. In the Americas it falls in the afternoon or midday of January 7. In Europe and Africa it is the same evening.

Traditionally, a new moon is a "fresh start." Many people write intentions in the first 24 to 48 hours. Capricorn adds a practical rule: keep them small enough to measure.

How to Write Intentions That Actually Work

  1. Pick one area: work, money, health or home.
  2. Make it measurable: "Save $200 a month" beats "be better with money."
  3. Add a date: choose a check-in on the first full moon, January 22.
  4. Name the first step: what can you do in the next 15 minutes?
  5. Write it by hand: it takes longer and helps you think.

Journal Prompts for the New Moon

  • What did I build in 2026 that I want to keep building?
  • Which habit costs me the most time or energy?
  • What would "enough" look like in my work this year?
  • Who do I trust to keep me honest about my goals?
  • What is one thing I will stop doing in 2027?

Why January Resolutions Fail (and What Capricorn Energy Teaches)

Behavior researchers often note that most resolutions fade within weeks. The reason is rarely a lack of willpower. The goals are too big, too vague or have no first step. Capricorn's "small, measurable, scheduled" approach lines up well with what habit science suggests.

  • Choose one habit change at a time.
  • Attach it to something you already do, like coffee or brushing your teeth.
  • Track it on paper. A visible streak keeps you honest.
  • Plan for a bad week. Decide in advance what "minimum" looks like.

You do not need astrology to use these tips. The season is simply a useful calendar prompt.

Should You Get a Reading This Season?

Many people book a birth chart reading or a "year ahead" forecast at the start of the year. It can be a nice reflective exercise, but it is entertainment and self-reflection, not a guarantee of anything. Never let a reader pressure you into big money or life decisions.

If you want a personal reading, take your time choosing who you work with. A careful astrologer will ask for your exact birth time and explain what the reading covers before you pay.

How to Choose a Good Astrologer

  • Look for detailed reviews, not just five stars and "amazing."
  • Check what you get: a PDF, a voice note or a video, and how long it is.
  • Give your exact birth time if you know it. Without it, house readings are guesses.
  • Avoid fear-based sellers who mention curses or urgent paid fixes.
  • Set a budget before you book.

Capricorn Season vs. the Sky: A Quick Fact Check

Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, which follows the seasons, not the star patterns. Because Earth's axis wobbles slowly (a movement called precession), the Sun is not actually in front of the Capricorn constellation for most of "Capricorn season." In late December it is crossing Sagittarius, and in mid January it moves toward Capricornus.

Tropical astrology ties the signs to the solstices and equinoxes on purpose. That is why Capricorn season always begins at the December solstice. When does Capricorn season end?

On Wednesday, January 20, 2027, at 07:30 UTC, when the Sun enters Aquarius. On the US West Coast that is the evening of January 19.

When is the New Moon in Capricorn in January 2027?

On January 7, 2027, at 20:24 UTC (3:24 p.m. Eastern, 12:24 p.m. Pacific), at about 17 degrees of Capricorn.

Is Mercury retrograde during Capricorn season?

No. Mercury enters Capricorn on December 25 and Aquarius on January 13. Mars, not Mercury, turns retrograde on January 10, 2027.

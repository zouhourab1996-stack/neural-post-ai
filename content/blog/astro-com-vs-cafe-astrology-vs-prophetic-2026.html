Astro.com — the professional's chart generator

Astro.com (run by the Swiss company Astrodienst) is the most cited free astrology site on the internet for a reason. It uses the Swiss Ephemeris — the same calculation engine that powers professional astrology software worldwide — and produces charts with precision down to roughly 0.001 arcseconds. If you need a technically correct Western tropical birth chart, this is where the precision lives.

The site draws over 8 million monthly visitors and has been trusted by professional astrologers since 1996. It offers free natal charts, daily and yearly horoscopes, a full ephemeris, and in-depth articles by well-known astrologers including Liz Greene and Robert Hand. Advanced techniques — progressions, solar returns, composite charts — are all available.

What Astro.com does well

  • Highest-precision free Western tropical chart calculations available online
  • Advanced techniques: progressions, solar returns, composite and synastry charts
  • World-class educational articles by named, respected astrologers
  • Available in 12 languages
  • No signup required for the core free tools

What Astro.com does not do

  • No written interpretation of your specific chart. You get the chart — the wheel, the table of positions — and then you are on your own. The site publishes articles that explain concepts, but it does not take your birth data and write you a reading.
  • Dated interface. The visual design is functional and decades-old. It is not hostile, but it is not the easiest first experience for someone who has never read a chart before.
  • Premium upsell. The free tools are genuinely free, but the site also sells detailed reports from named astrologers at $20–$65+, which is fine — just know that the free tier ends where interpretation begins.
Good to know: Astro.com is the right choice when your question is technical — "where exactly is my Venus right now?" or "what does my progressed chart look like?" It is not the right choice when your question is interpretive — "what does this actually mean for me?" — unless you are comfortable cross-referencing the chart against articles yourself.

Cafe Astrology — the free encyclopedia

Cafe Astrology has been a staple of the online astrology community since 2002, run by astrologer Annie. Its commitment to being completely free with no paywall has not changed in over two decades. The site offers free natal chart reports, synastry and composite reports, transit reports, daily and yearly horoscopes, and — most distinctively — an enormous library of educational articles.

If you have ever searched "Venus in Scorpio meaning" or "what does my Sun sign say about me" and landed on a long, plainly-written page that explained the placement in detail, there is a very good chance that page was on Cafe Astrology. The site ranks for nearly every astrology question on Google, and for good reason: it has thousands of articles covering signs, houses, aspects, transits, and compatibility.

What Cafe Astrology does well

  • Massive free article library — possibly the largest collection of free astrology writing on the web
  • Free natal, synastry, and composite chart reports with written interpretations
  • Decanate-based horoscopes, which are more specific than most sign-only readings
  • Excellent for self-study and learning Western astrology from scratch
  • No signup, no paywall, no hidden costs — genuinely free

What Cafe Astrology does not do

  • No personalization to your specific chart. The reports are template-based — they cover what Venus in Scorpio means in general, not what your Venus in Scorpio means given your full chart context, your house placements, your aspects. Good for learning, not for a personal reading.
  • Text-heavy, no interactive chart. The site is a library, not a tool. You read articles and you get a static report — there is no interactive chart you can explore, no hover-to-identify, no visual layer beyond what the report delivers.
  • Web-only, no mobile app. All of it lives in a browser. Navigation can feel overwhelming because of the sheer volume of content.
Good to know: Cafe Astrology is the best first stop when you want to learn — when your question is "what does this placement mean?" rather than "what does this mean for me specifically?" Pair it with Astro.com for the chart itself, and you have a solid free foundation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Astro.com or Cafe Astrology more accurate?

For chart calculation, Astro.com is more accurate — it uses the Swiss Ephemeris with arc-second precision, and that is the professional standard. For written interpretation of general meanings, both are informative; Cafe Astrology has more articles. But "accuracy" in astrology has two layers: the calculation (where Astro.com wins) and the interpretation (where neither free site personalizes to your chart, and a professional consultation is a different question).

Is Cafe Astrology really completely free?

Yes — Cafe Astrology has been completely free with no paywall since 2002. There are no premium tiers, no signup walls, and no locked content. Astro.com's core chart tools are also free, but the site sells premium reports from named astrologers at $20–$65+.

Can I use Astro.com and Cafe Astrology together?

That is actually the best free combination available: use Astro.com to generate your precise chart, then use Cafe Astrology's articles to understand what each placement means. The gap is that neither site connects the two — Cafe Astrology's reports are template-based and do not refer to your specific chart, and Astro.com does not provide interpretation at all.

Does Prophetic replace either of these sites?

No — Prophetic does not replace Astro.com's calculation precision or Cafe Astrology's article volume. What it does is combine a calculation layer with written interpretation in the same place, add interactive tools beyond static reports, and serve Arabic-speaking readers — none of which the other two do together. For technical chart work, Astro.com remains the gold standard. For the largest free article library, Cafe Astrology remains unmatched.

Which site is best for a beginner?

Cafe Astrology is the most beginner-friendly for learning — the articles start from the basics and explain terminology as they go. Astro.com's charts are precise but assume you know how to read them. Prophetic sits between the two: it is written to be accessible, and it connects the chart to interpretation, but it is a smaller library than Cafe Astrology. A good beginner path is Cafe Astrology for learning, Astro.com for the chart, and Prophetic as the place that brings both together.

Are these sites reliable, or is astrology just pseudoscience?

Astrology is not a science in the empirical sense — there is no peer-reviewed consensus that planetary positions cause personality traits or life events. What the sites above offer is a long cultural and interpretive tradition, presented honestly. Astro.com and Cafe Astrology are reliable in the sense that their calculations are technically correct and their articles accurately represent astrological interpretations. Prophetic follows the same honest approach: it computes dates from data rather than copying them, and it presents astrological interpretations as interpretations — "astrologers read this as…" — rather than as established fact.

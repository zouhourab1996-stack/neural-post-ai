Sagittarius Season 2026 at a Glance

Sagittarius season 2026 begins on Sunday, November 22, at 07:23 UTC, when the Sun moves out of Scorpio and into Sagittarius. It ends on Monday, December 21, at 20:50 UTC, the exact moment of the December solstice, when the Sun enters Capricorn.

If you have felt stuck through October and early November, you are not imagining the mood that astrologers describe. Mercury and Venus spent weeks retrograde in the fall, and both turned direct around November 13 and 14. Sagittarius season is the first stretch after that where the sky, in astrological terms, "moves forward" again. It is a good time to plan, learn, travel, and say yes to bigger ideas.

This guide gives you the exact times in your time zone, every key date in the season, what the season can mean for your rising sign, and a simple plan you can actually use. No scare stories, no fake promises.

Quick answer

  • Starts: November 22, 2026, 07:23 UTC (2:23 a.m. Eastern; 11:23 p.m. Pacific on November 21).
  • Ends: December 21, 2026, 20:50 UTC (winter solstice in the Northern Hemisphere).
  • Biggest dates: Full Moon in Gemini on November 24, New Moon in Sagittarius on December 9, Jupiter retrograde on December 13.
  • Theme: big picture, honesty, learning, travel and optimism, with a reminder to check the details.

The New Moon in Sagittarius on December 9

This is the heart of the season. The New Moon falls at 16°56' Sagittarius at about 00:51 UTC on December 9. In the Americas, that is still the evening of December 8. In Europe and Africa, it is the very early hours of December 9.

Astrologers usually treat a new moon as a "seed" moment. A Sagittarius new moon is linked to questions like: What do I want to learn next year? Where do I want to go? What belief is holding me back? It is a useful date to write down one or two goals for 2027, even if you don't follow astrology closely. A clear goal on paper helps whatever the sky is doing.

Jupiter Turns Retrograde on December 13

Jupiter is the traditional ruler of Sagittarius, so its retrograde in the middle of the season stands out. It stays retrograde in Leo until about April 13, 2027. In astrological language, a Jupiter retrograde asks you to review how you grow rather than to rush into new expansion. Think: finish the course you started, before signing up for three more. What Sagittarius Season Means in Astrology

Sagittarius is a fire sign ruled by Jupiter and symbolized by the archer, half human and half horse. In traditional astrology, it is the sign of the long journey, both outer travel and inner searching. Here are the themes most astrologers connect with this season:

  • Big-picture thinking. Less "how do I get through this week" and more "where is my life going?"
  • Learning and teaching. Courses, books, languages, mentors and sharing what you know.
  • Travel and new places. The season lines up with holiday travel in many countries.
  • Honesty. Sagittarius is famous for saying the quiet part out loud. Kind honesty works better than blunt honesty.
  • Optimism and faith. A lighter mood after the intensity of Scorpio season.

"Sagittarius season works best when you aim high and then check the small print."

The shadow side is real too. Astrologers warn about overpromising, overspending during the holidays, and ignoring details because the big idea feels exciting. With Mars in Virgo from November 25, there is a built-in balance: dream big, then make a checklist.

Fire Signs (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius)

This is your element, so many astrologers see the season as a confidence boost. Aries risings may feel the travel bug. Leo risings get a creative and romantic month. Sagittarius risings get a personal new year, which makes the December 9 new moon especially worth noting.

Earth Signs (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn)

Earth signs often find Sagittarius energy a bit restless. The useful move is to put that energy into something concrete. Taurus risings can sort out shared finances. Virgo risings can make home more comfortable for winter. Capricorn risings benefit from rest before their own season starts on December 21.

Air Signs (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius)

Air and fire get along well. Gemini risings feel the focus on partnerships, and the November 24 full moon falls in their own sign. Libra risings may write, pitch or reach out more. Aquarius risings are pulled toward community, events and group projects.

Water Signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces)

After Scorpio season, water signs may welcome a lighter month. Cancer risings can reset daily habits. Scorpio risings get a practical money focus. Pisces risings may see career news, especially around the new moon.

Good to know: These are traditional symbolic readings. There is no reliable scientific evidence that the Sun's sign changes events in your life. Many people use the season as a prompt for reflection and planning, and that is the healthiest way to use it.

Sagittarius Season vs. the Sagittarius Constellation

Here is a fact many horoscope sites skip. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, which follows the seasons, not the star patterns. Because of a slow wobble of Earth's axis called precession, the Sun is not actually in front of the Sagittarius constellation for most of "Sagittarius season." In late November it is still crossing Scorpius and Ophiuchus, and it reaches the Sagittarius star group only around December 18.

This is not a mistake by astrologers. Tropical astrology ties the signs to the solstices and equinoxes on purpose. Sagittarius season always ends at the December solstice, and that is why its timing is so reliable from year to year.

New Moon in Capricorn, January 2027: The Short Answer

The first new moon of 2027 is exact on Thursday, January 7, 2027, at 20:24 UTC. The Sun and Moon meet at about 17 degrees of Capricorn. In New York that is 3:24 p.m., and in Los Angeles it is 12:24 p.m.

Astrologers read a Capricorn new moon as a time to start something slow and solid: a career step, a budget, a habit or a long-term plan. Below you will find the time in your zone, the timeline around it, and a house-by-house guide for every sun sign.

Key takeaways

  • Exact time: January 7, 2027, 20:24 UTC (3:24 p.m. ET, 12:24 p.m. PT).
  • Sign and degree: Capricorn, about 17 degrees 18 minutes.
  • It is not an eclipse. The next eclipse new moon is February 6, 2027.
  • Theme: structure, discipline, patience and one realistic goal.
  • Full moon follows on January 22 (Wolf Moon in Leo, supermoon).

You cannot see a new moon. It sits between Earth and the Sun with its lit side turned away. The sky is darkest around this date, which makes it a good week for stargazing. You can confirm the date in the US Naval Observatory moon phase table.

What a New Moon in Capricorn Means

New moons mark the start of a lunar cycle of about 29.5 days. The sign tells you the flavor. Capricorn is an earth sign ruled by Saturn, linked to work, structure and responsibility.

That is why this new moon is often called the goal-setting new moon. It does not reward hype. It rewards a plan with a date on it.

Good uses of this energy

  • Writing a 6-month plan with three checkpoints.
  • Setting a budget or a savings target.
  • Starting a career or study step.
  • Decluttering a workspace or your calendar.

Honest note: there is no scientific evidence that the Moon's sign changes your results. Use the date as a calm, symbolic start line. The progress still comes from your actions.

A Simple New Moon Ritual (15 Minutes)

  1. Choose a calm time within a day of 20:24 UTC. The exact minute is not required.
  2. Write one to three goals in the present tense and make each measurable.
  3. Next to each goal, write the first action you can take within 48 hours.
  4. Put a review date on the calendar for the full moon on January 22.
  5. Close the page and take the first action the same week.

Common Mistakes With New Moon Goals

  • Too many goals. Three is a safe limit.
  • Vague wording. "Be healthier" is hard to track. "Walk 20 minutes, 4 days a week" is clear.
  • Waiting for the exact minute. The days around the date are fine.
  • Skipping the review. The full moon two weeks later is a natural check-in.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the new moon in January 2027?

On Thursday, January 7, 2027, at 20:24 UTC. That is 3:24 p.m. Eastern and 12:24 p.m. Pacific on January 7. In much of Asia and Australia it is already January 8.

What sign is the January 2027 new moon in?

Capricorn, at about 17 degrees 18 minutes. The Sun and Moon meet in the same sign, as they do at every new moon.

Is the January 2027 new moon an eclipse?

No. The next eclipse is the annular solar eclipse at the February 6, 2027 new moon in Aquarius.

What should I do on the Capricorn new moon?

Write one to three goals you can act on within 48 hours, then take the first small step. Capricorn favors plans with dates and deadlines over big promises.

When is the next full moon after it?

The Wolf Moon full moon in Leo is on January 22, 2027, at 12:17 UTC. It is also listed as a supermoon.

Can the new moon predict my year?

No. Astrology is a tool for reflection and entertainment, not a scientific prediction. Use the date as a reason to plan, and rely on your own effort.

When Does Scorpio Season 2026 Start and End?

Scorpio season starts at the exact moment the Sun reaches 0° Scorpio, which is 210° along the ecliptic, counted from the March equinox point. That moment happens once, at the same instant everywhere on Earth. Only the clock time changes with your location.

Two independent sources agree on the start time within one minute: Spiritclick's zodiac season table lists 09:37 UTC, and Astro-Seek's October 2026 calendar lists 09:38. Our own calculation with NASA's DE421 planetary data gives 09:37. For the end, our figure of 07:23 UTC matches Oraclume; Spiritclick rounds it to 07:20.

Important note on clocks: two clock changes fall inside this season. The UK goes back to GMT on Sunday, October 25. The US and Canada leave daylight time on Sunday, November 1. That is why the start is listed in EDT/PDT/BST and the end in EST/PST/GMT.

So does it end November 21 or 22? Both are honest. On Pacific time it ends on the night of the 21st; in Eastern time and the UK, early on the 22nd.

Libra or Scorpio? The Cusp Checker

Here is the part most guides skip. The Sun does not enter Scorpio on the same date and hour every year. Our calendar has 365 days, the Sun's trip around the zodiac takes about 365.24, and leap years reset the drift. So the switch slides by roughly six hours a year, then jumps back. Someone born on October 23 at 8 AM in New York was a Scorpio in 2018 but a Libra in 2019.

What Scorpio Season Means

In Western astrology, Scorpio is the fixed water sign. "Fixed" means it sits in the middle of a season, when things are settled and deep. "Water" links it to feelings, bonds and memory. Its traditional ruler is Mars. Modern astrologers add Pluto, the planet named after the god of the underworld.

Put those together and you get the classic Scorpio themes:

  • Depth over small talk. Fewer, more honest conversations.
  • Shared resources. Joint money, debt, taxes, inheritances, and who owes what to whom.
  • Intimacy and trust. Who you let all the way in, and who you don't.
  • Endings that make room. Letting go of a habit, a grudge or a commitment that has run its course.
  • Research and focus. Digging until you find the real answer.

There is also a simple seasonal logic to it. In the Northern Hemisphere, Scorpio season covers the stretch when days shorten fastest and the trees drop their leaves. Many cultures mark this time with festivals for the dead: Samhain, All Saints' Day, the Day of the Dead. The sign's reputation for endings and the hidden grew up in that landscape.

"What we do not make conscious emerges later as fate." A popular paraphrase of an idea from Carl Jung, often quoted during Scorpio season

You don't need to believe a planet shapes your mood to find that useful. A month of looking under the surface of your life is a good habit either way.

Why This Scorpio Season Feels Different

A normal Scorpio season is the Sun moving through the sign and not much else. In 2026 the Sun walks into a crowded room.

Venus is already retrograde

Venus turned retrograde in Scorpio on October 3 and stays that way until the evening of November 13 in America (November 14 in GMT). Astrologers link Venus to love, money and what we value. Within a day of the season opening, the Sun lines up exactly with Venus. Astrologers call this the "cazimi," the heart of the retrograde. Our Venus retrograde 2026 guide covers the full cycle, shadow dates included.

Mercury joins it the next morning

Mercury, the planet of messages and plans, turns retrograde at 20°58′ Scorpio on October 24. So from the second day of the season, both planets of love and of communication are moving backward. If you want the sign-by-sign version, see Mercury retrograde in Scorpio for all 12 signs.

Both turn direct on the same day

The double retrograde ends on November 13, when Mercury stations direct in the morning and Venus follows that evening (Americas time). That makes the second half of the season, November 14 to 22, the cleanest window to restart things.

Reality check: retrogrades are optical effects. The planets do not reverse. Earth overtakes or is overtaken, so they appear to move backward against the stars. No study has shown that these periods break phones or ruin relationships. Treat them as a cue to double-check, not a reason to panic.

A Week-by-Week Guide to Scorpio Season 2026

Week 1 (Oct 23–29): The crowded opening

The season starts with the Venus cazimi, the Mercury station and then the Hunter's Moon in Taurus on the night of October 25–26, which squares Pluto. Symbolically, it is a week about money, ownership and control. Practically: slow down on big purchases, save drafts instead of hitting send, and let heated topics cool overnight.

Week 2 (Oct 30 – Nov 5): Halloween and the review

Halloween arrives mid-retrograde with the Sun at 8° Scorpio. On November 4 the Sun meets Mercury, which astrologers read as the moment the "review" becomes clear. Good week for sorting old files, closing old accounts and finishing half-done tasks.

Week 3 (Nov 6–12): The fresh start you shouldn't rush

The real cross-quarter day lands on November 7, and the New Moon in Scorpio follows on November 9. New moons are the traditional time to set intentions. With both planets still retrograde, write the intention down but wait a few days to act. The 11/11 date falls two days later, if you like numerology.

Week 4 (Nov 13–22): Green light, with one caution

Mercury and Venus turn direct on the 13th. Mars meets Jupiter in Leo on November 16, and the Sun squares both of them on the 18th and 19th. Astrologers read that as big energy with a risk of overdoing it. Start the thing, but keep promises and spending realistic. Mars itself slows down soon after; our Mars retrograde 2027 guide explains what follows over the winter.

Halloween vs. the Real Samhain Point

Halloween grew out of Samhain, a Celtic festival that marked the start of the dark half of the year. Old calendars placed it roughly halfway between the autumn equinox and the winter solstice. These "cross-quarter" days were later fixed to calendar dates, which is why we celebrate on October 31.

If you measure by the Sun instead of the calendar, the halfway point is when the Sun reaches 15° Scorpio. In 2026 that happens on Saturday, November 7 at 4:52 AM ET (09:52 GMT). Some modern pagans and astrologers mark this "astronomical Samhain" as a quieter second celebration.

The Real Sky: Where the Sun Actually Is

Here is a fact that surprises a lot of people. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac, twelve equal 30° slices measured from the March equinox. The constellations with the same names are star patterns of very different sizes, and they have drifted out of step over about two thousand years because Earth's axis slowly wobbles (precession).

We checked the Sun's position against the official constellation borders set by the International Astronomical Union in 1930. In 2026:

Those numbers line up with Britannica, which notes the Sun spends only about a week in Scorpius, the shortest stay of any zodiac constellation, and with EarthSky's Ophiuchus explainer.

Does this mean your sign is "wrong"? No. Tropical astrology never claimed to follow the constellations. It follows the seasons. Scorpio season is "the second month of autumn," and that stays true no matter where the stars sit. Sidereal (Vedic) astrology uses a different system, which is why it often gives a different sign.

How to Use Scorpio Season (and What to Skip)

Worth doing

  1. A money check-in. List your debts, shared bills and subscriptions. Scorpio's "shared resources" theme is a good excuse.
  2. One honest conversation. Pick one, not five. Write down what you want to say first.
  3. A clear-out. Delete old files, unfollow accounts that make you feel worse, donate what you don't use.
  4. A remembrance evening. Around November 1–7, light a candle, look at old photos, cook a loved one's recipe.
  5. Sleep. Darker evenings are a natural nudge to rest. (Curious about the moon's effect? We looked at what science says about the full moon and sleep.)

Worth skipping

  • Rushing a big decision just because "the stars said so." No sky event replaces real advice for money, health or law.
  • Reading every jealous thought as intuition. Scorpio season can make people suspicious. Check the facts first.
  • Spending money on "retrograde protection" products. A calendar reminder to double-check your plans works just as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

When does Scorpio season start in 2026?

On Friday, October 23, 2026, at 09:37 GMT. That is 5:37 AM Eastern, 2:37 AM Pacific and 10:37 AM in the UK.

When does Scorpio season end in 2026?

I was born on October 23. Am I a Libra or a Scorpio?

Is Mercury retrograde during Scorpio season 2026?

Yes. Mercury is retrograde from October 24 to November 13, 2026, and Venus is retrograde until the evening of November 13 in the Americas (November 14 GMT).

Is the Sun really in the Scorpio constellation during Scorpio season?

No. In 2026 it sits in front of Virgo and then Libra for most of the season. It only crosses the Scorpius constellation from about November 23 to 30, after Scorpio season ends. Western astrology follows the seasons, not the star patterns.

What is the best day of Scorpio season 2026 to start something new?

Many astrologers would set an intention at the New Moon on November 9 and take action after November 13, when both Mercury and Venus turn direct. Treat it as a planning tip, not a rule.

The budget bands that make sense

The cheapest listing is rarely the cheapest project. A low headline price may exclude captions, source files, commercial music, revisions or vertical exports. Compare the complete deliverable, not the thumbnail price. For a first order, a small clip is more informative than a large package with a discount.

  • Small test: one short clip with one revision.
  • Working package: several clips from the same source and a written style brief.
  • Monthly production: only after a paid pilot proves the editor understands your voice, pacing and audience.

Five checks before ordering

  1. Ask whether the seller uses your footage, licensed stock or generated assets.
  2. Confirm whether the voice, music, images and AI outputs have commercial-use rights.
  3. Specify aspect ratios: 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 are different exports.
  4. Define captions, hook style, number of revisions and delivery format.
  5. Keep payment and messages on Fiverr; never trade platform protection for a private discount.
Do not buy a promise of virality. An editor can improve the cut, hook and packaging. Nobody can guarantee views, followers or revenue.

How to read reviews like a buyer

Ignore a wall of “amazing” reviews. Look for buyers who mention the actual work: pacing, captions, turnaround, source files and whether revisions were respected. Open the three-star reviews first. They often reveal what the package does not include.
